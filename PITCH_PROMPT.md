# PSB SecureWealth Twin — Pitch Prompt & Content Deck

## 1. Hook
**SecureWealth Twin is an AI financial twin that grows your wealth and protects every rupee before a risky action happens.**

## 2. Problem Statement
- Social-engineering fraud, coerced transactions, OTP misuse, impulsive high-value actions, fragmented wealth tools.
- Banks must help customers invest more **and** stay safe — without friction.

## 3. Solution
Two inseparable halves:
1. **Wealth Intelligence Twin** — learns income, spending, goals, assets, market signals.
2. **Mandatory Wealth Protection Layer** — risk check before every critical action: **Allow / Warn+Cool / Block**.

## 4. Hero Features

### SecureWealth Twin AI
- Conversational AI, live PSB FD rates, FD ladder, Monte Carlo projections, explainable recommendations.
- **Data:** Best PSB FD 7.5% (general) / 8.0% (senior); ladder 1Y 7.25%, 2Y 7.35%, 3Y 7.5%, 5Y 7.25%.

### Bhavishya AI
- 24+ prediction engines, ETB/NTB segmentation, cold-start AI for new customers with no transaction history.
- **Data:** 174 signals analysed, 94.2% historical accuracy, 58% → 72% cold-start confidence after income declared.

### Behavioural Speed / Biometrics
- Optional speed pattern, HD Face ID with oval guide + auto-capture, behavioural anomaly detection.
- **Data:** 72% quality threshold, ~67% reduction in impulsive fraud (demo metric).

### Fraud Protection
- 6-layer scan, Low/Medium/High risk score, cooling vault, collective immune system.
- **Data:** 2.8M+ immune users, 847,293 protected today, 47 active clusters.

### Admin Fraud Intelligence Center
- India-only map, case explorer, trace network, alert correlation.
- **Data:** 2 active trails, 1 critical risk, ₹40.2L INR traced (demo).

## 5. Tech Stack
React 19 + Vite + Tailwind + Capacitor (Android) | Node.js/Express backend | SQLite + WebSocket | face-api.js + rules/LLM AI | Render + GitHub Actions auto-APK | Free custom OTA via `/mobile/bundle` endpoints.
